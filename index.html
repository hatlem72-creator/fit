Import from Huawei Health

Pick the .json files from a Huawei data export, or a CSV file. Existing entries for the same day are updated, not duplicated.

How do I get the export?
  1. On your phone go to Settings → Huawei ID (your name) → Privacy centre → Request your data (in some versions: Huawei Health → Me → Privacy management).
  2. Tick Health, set a password for the ZIP and submit. Huawei emails you a download link (can take a few hours up to a few days).
  3. Download and unzip it (Files app, with your password).
  4. Here, choose these files:
    Sport per minute merged data & description / sport per minute merged data.json (steps + active calories per day) and
    Motion path detail data & description / motion path detail data.json (workouts).

CSV works too, with a header row like:
date,active_kcal,total_kcal,steps,workout,minutes

Huawei has no live web connection for the Watch Fit 4 Pro, so daily copying (≈10 seconds) is the quickest way. Tip: do it in the evening.

🔒 Privacy & backup

Everything is stored only in this browser on this device (IndexedDB). Nothing is sent to a server; only barcode look-ups ask Open Food Facts about the barcode number. Make a backup now and then, and use it to move to a new phone.

The PIN is a privacy screen for this app on this device. It's stored as a salted hash. Forgot it? Clear the site data in your browser (you'd lose data without a backup).

❓ Help

Add to home screen

Android/Chrome: menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app. iPhone/Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen. It works offline afterwards.

Scanning a nutrition label

Tap ＋ (New food) → the small 📷 Scan label button. Photograph the nutrition table straight on, filling the frame, with good light. The app reads it on your phone (English, Dutch, Norwegian), fills in calories, protein, fat and carbs for the amount you enter (100 g if empty), and shows the photo next to them so you can check and correct them before saving. The first scan downloads the reading engine (~15 MB) once; after that it works offline.

How the target is calculated

BMR uses the Mifflin-St Jeor formula. TDEE = BMR × an activity factor from your training sessions per week (0: 1.2, 1–2: 1.375, 3–4: 1.55, 5–6: 1.725, 7+: 1.9). Losing or gaining 1 kg ≈ 7700 kcal, so 0.5 kg/week = ±550 kcal/day. The target never goes below 1500 kcal (men) / 1200 kcal (women). Protein defaults to 2.0 g/kg when losing, 1.6 maintaining and 1.8 gaining; fat is 27% of calories; carbs fill the rest.

Manual targets, ranges & habits

With Manual targets on, your daily intake target is fixed (e.g. 1,870 kcal) and the protein / carb / fat goals are ranges. Fat can be auto-calculated from what's left of the calories (mid-point ± 5 g). On Today the green band on each macro bar is your range: the bar is green inside it, amber below and red above. Watch calories still show as burnt / balance but don't change the target. Habits tick themselves when steps reach the goal or a Rowing workout of enough minutes is logged; you can also tick them by hand. The streak counts days with both habits done.

Burnt calories

If you enter total calories from the watch, that's used. If you only enter active calories, burnt = BMR + active. With nothing entered, the estimated TDEE is shown (marked “est.”).
